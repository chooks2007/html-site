ABOUT, GAMING at NovaForge Studios

NovaForge Studios was founded in 2021, Braamfontein, Johannesburg in Gauteng

We are mainly a gaming company or organization. We do things such games get sold, playing games etc.

Our mission

Our end goal is to create mostly entertaining and memorable video games that inspire creativity, connect players around the world and to deliver exceptional gaming experiences through the form of the quality of the games.

Not only do we sell consoles, games etc. We also host things such as giveaways and tournaments those being our main events whereby if you were to win a tournament then you would win a prize.

Our vision

To become a globally known game development company known for producing groundbreaking games that shape the future of interactive entertainment and just brings a community together.

Target audience

Teenagers and young adults mainly between the ages of (13-35). Dedicated gamers. Players on consoles such as Xbox, PlayStation, Pc and Nintendo Switch.
